# Landing artwork

`porsche-993-alpine.webp` is original generated promotional artwork of a silver
1995 Porsche 911 Turbo (993) on an Alpine road. It is an illustration, not a
gameplay screenshot or an image extracted from the original game. Generated
with the built-in image generation tool and exported to WebP for the website.

copyright applies. Porsche names, vehicle designs and trademarks remain subject
to their owners' rights; no affiliation or endorsement is implied.
